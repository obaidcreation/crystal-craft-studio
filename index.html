FAQ

Before you order

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp and we will reply with photos.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes. You pay when the parcel reaches you, anywhere in Pakistan.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is free on orders above Rs. 3,000. For smaller orders, we confirm the delivery charge on WhatsApp before dispatch.

Can I place a custom order?

Yes. Send us the colour, size and design you have in mind. We confirm the price and ready date before we start.

How do I order?

Tap "Order on WhatsApp" under any piece, or fill in the form below. We reply with photos, the final price and delivery details.

How should I care for my jewelry?

Keep pieces away from water, put on perfume before your jewelry, and store each piece in a soft pouch.

Where are you based?

Our studio is in Attock City, Punjab. We ship to every city in Pakistan.
